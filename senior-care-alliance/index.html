No tech skills needed—just type what you’re looking for. Want facilities with clean compliance records? SNFs with fewer complaints? You ask, we show. It’s a smarter way to scan the entire market in seconds.

Tag Your Insureds. Uncover What’s Driving Loss Trends and Profitability.

Quickly identify the facilities you already service – or want to pursue. Create custom portfolios by region, risk score, Loss Runs, or any metric that matters to you. Instantly find trends, compare performance, flag risks, and uncover growth opportunities.

This tool lets you create and analyze your own facility portfolio in two flexible ways: Stage 1 – Explore Publicly: Start by selecting from our 15,000+ skilled nursing facilities to build a custom portfolio — your active clients, your target facility portfolio, or your regional footprint. Interrogate your facility book for loss exposure patterns, ask questions, compare risk profiles, and uncover meaningful insights.

Stage 2 – Upload Privately (Optional): When you’re ready, you can upload a simple list — just the names or CCNs of the facilities you insure, manage, or underwrite. Whether it’s 2 or 2,000 facilities, you’ll be able to chat with it, benchmark it, and surface opportunities across your entire portfolio.
